## Integration Lifecycle

1. **API Key Generation & Webhook Configuration**: Verifier organizations configure endpoints in the PrivPass developer portal with AES-256-GCM encrypted HMAC credentials.
2. **Selective Disclosure Request Formulation**: Partners define minimal boolean queries (`verifyAgeOver18`, `verifyPanFormat`, `verifyResidency`).
3. **User Flow**: End users scan partner QR codes, execute Compact ZK circuits locally via Lace or 1AM, and authorize proof dispatch.
4. **On-Chain Settlement**: Proof verified on Midnight Preprod smart contract (`IdentityVerification`) without exposing private identifiers.
